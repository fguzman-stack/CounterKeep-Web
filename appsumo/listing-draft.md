## FAQ
Q: Does CounterKeep need the internet for daily use?
A: Core sales, inventory and reports use a local desktop database, so an internet connection is not required for those operations
Q: Which operating systems are supported?
A: The current desktop build targets Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit, with Microsoft WebView2
Q: Where are business records stored?
A: The desktop app stores business records in a local SQLite database
Q: Does it process card payments?
A: No, it records the selected payment method but does not process card transactions or integrate with payment terminals
Q: Is certified electronic tax invoicing included?
A: No certified electronic tax invoicing integration is included, so verify your local fiscal requirements before using generated documents for official purposes
Q: Can I export my data?
A: Product and contact lists support CSV/XLSX exports, documents and reports support PDF, and settings can export a .ckbackup file; a complete restore workflow is not yet available
Q: Which interface languages are available?
A: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French and German are available in the interface
