What activation receives

Your Gumroad key and purchase email and the app-derived device identifier are sent over HTTPS to the activation service hosted on Supabase. The service verifies the key with Gumroad without incrementing Gumroad's use counter. Valid purchases receive a signed license compatible with the app.

The service stores a derived credential hash and license record, including the purchase email and derived device identifier, to support recovery, binding and revocation. The raw Gumroad key is not stored in the licensing database or browser storage by this page. Temporary download URLs exist only in browser memory. The license file contains your email: keep it private.

Business data and external services

This activation flow does not receive business documents, sales records or procedures. Supabase, GitHub Pages and Gumroad may process technical request metadata under their own policies. The application can contact external providers if you enable those features; their own conditions apply.

Abuse prevention and refunds

Rate limiting uses a salted hash of the network address. Recovery uses the same key and email and the same bound device. Refund/dispute notifications are rechecked against Gumroad before revoking the corresponding license; an external-service outage is not treated as a refund. License records are retained to maintain purchase rights and support. Contact the publisher for access, correction or deletion requests, identity verification, email mistakes or device transfers.
